Who we are

Building sustainable value from the ground up.

Delight Veld (Pvt) Ltd is a Zimbabwean land development company specialising in land acquisition, urban planning, infrastructure development, serviced stands and property investment.

Our approach combines sound planning principles, engineering capability, regulatory awareness and customer-focused service to create developments that support modern living and long-term economic growth.

Our Vision

To become one of Zimbabwe’s leading land development companies, recognised for sustainable planning, quality infrastructure and developments that create lasting value.

Our Mission

To transform land into well-planned communities through professional planning, reliable infrastructure, responsible development and customer-focused service.

Core Values

Integrity, professionalism, innovation, sustainability, excellence, accountability and customer satisfaction.

Our services

Integrated land and infrastructure solutions.

From early feasibility and planning to construction, servicing and property sales, our services support the full development journey.

Land Development

Land acquisition and development, residential and commercial layouts, mixed-use developments, gated community planning and servicing of residential stands.

Land Surveys & Stand Pegging

Professional land surveying, topographical assessment, stand-boundary pegging and development support for residential, commercial and institutional projects.

Earthmoving & Roadworks

Earthmoving equipment services, road grading, road clearing, access-road preparation and road construction for development projects.

Civil Infrastructure

Road design, sewer reticulation, stormwater drainage, water-supply systems and coordination of essential infrastructure services.

Urban & Architectural Planning

Subdivision and consolidation schemes, master planning, site planning, building-plan design, permit applications and regulatory approval support.

Building Construction

Residential and commercial construction services, project coordination and practical delivery support from plans to completed structures.

Property Services

Land and property sales, valuations, estimations, property investment opportunities, feasibility studies and development consultancy.

Our development process

A clear path from land identification to project handover.

Delight Veld coordinates the technical, planning and commercial stages required to turn land into functioning developments.

  1. 01

    Land Identification & Acquisition

    Identify suitable land and assess the development opportunity.

  2. 02

    Feasibility Studies

    Review site conditions, planning potential, infrastructure needs and project viability.

  3. 03

    Surveying & Planning

    Prepare surveys, layouts, subdivision plans and development concepts.

  4. 04

    Regulatory Approvals

    Support development-permit applications and relevant approval processes.

  5. 05

    Infrastructure Development

    Coordinate roads, water, sewer, drainage and essential services.

  6. 06

    Servicing, Marketing & Handover

    Prepare stands for the market, facilitate sales and complete project handover.
